The MMU uses page tables and the TLB to translate addresses; a page fault loads missing data from secondary storage. LRU exploits temporal locality but exact LRU is expensive, so systems often use approximations. When physical frames are scarce, a replacement policy chooses which page to evict before bringing in the faulting page.
